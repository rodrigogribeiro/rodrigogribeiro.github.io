I am a professor in the Department of Computing at the Universidade Federal de Ouro Preto (UFOP), Minas Gerais, Brazil, where I have been teaching since 2008.

I graduated in Computer Science from UFOP in 2005, and obtained my MSc (2007) and PhD (2013) from the Universidade Federal de Minas Gerais (UFMG), the latter under the supervision of [Carlos Camarão](http://homepages.dcc.ufmg.br/~camarao/). During 2016–2017 I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Universidade Federal de Pelotas (UFPel), working with André Rauber Du Bois.

### Research interests

My research lies broadly within the design and implementation of programming languages. I am particularly interested in building **correct-by-construction software** using type theory-based proof assistants such as [Agda](https://github.com/agda/agda), [Rocq (Coq)](https://rocq-prover.org/) and [Lean](https://lean-lang.org/). Recent topics include:

- **Formal language theory and parsing** — mechanized results on regular expressions, context-free grammars, pushdown automata and Parsing Expression Grammars (PEGs);
- **Semantics and type systems** — mechanized metatheory, type inference and type-directed program generation;
- **Program verification** — program logics, verification of low-level code and property-based testing;
- **Functional programming** — Haskell libraries and embedded domain-specific languages.

I am always looking for motivated students interested in these topics. Feel free to get in touch!
